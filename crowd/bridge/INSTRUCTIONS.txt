ALTTPR SNES9X - TIKTOK CROWD CONTROL SETUP
==========================================

Your TikTok LIVE viewers send gifts, likes, follows and shares, and those
change your game: hearts, rupees, bombs, inverted controls, ice physics,
cucco attacks, even a brand-new seed. Everything runs on the PC you
stream from.

How it fits together:

  TikTok LIVE --> TikFinity --> this bridge --> the game in your browser


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- A Windows PC (Mac/Linux work too, see the end).
- Node.js 20 or newer: https://nodejs.org  (the "LTS" download is fine)
- TikFinity (free): https://tikfinity.zerody.one
- The game: https://hugecrankhank.github.io/ALTTPR-Snes9x-Beta/
- This folder, unzipped somewhere easy, like your Desktop.


ONE-TIME SETUP
--------------
1. Install Node.js. Click Next through the installer, defaults are fine.

2. Install TikFinity, open it, and log in with your TikTok account.
   Its WebSocket / Events API port should be 21213 (that's its default).

3. Open the game in your browser on the same PC, load your ROM, then:
   ... menu  ->  Crowd effects
   - Turn on "TikTok effects on"
   - Bridge address must be:  ws://localhost:21214
   - If the browser asks to allow access to devices on your local
     network, click Allow.


EVERY TIME YOU STREAM
---------------------
1. Open TikFinity (logged in).
2. Double-click start-bridge.bat in this folder.
   A black window opens. You should see:
     bridge listening on ws://localhost:21214
     connected to TikFinity on port 21213
   Leave this window open while you stream.
3. Open the game. The window should add:  game connected (1 open)
4. Go live on TikTok. Gifts show up in the black window and on the game.


TEST BEFORE GOING LIVE
----------------------
In the game: ... menu -> Crowd effects has a test button for every effect.
Press one, close the menu, and it happens in-game.

"New seed!" only works after you tick "Allow seed reroll" in that menu.
It ends your current game: a banner counts down 10 seconds (with a Cancel
button in the menu), then makes and loads a new seed.


CHOOSING WHICH GIFT DOES WHAT
-----------------------------
Open mapping.json in Notepad. The "gifts" list is:  "Gift name": "effect"

  "Rose": "heal_1",
  "Rosa": "ice",
  "Cap": "cucco_storm",

Save the file and it reloads by itself, no restart needed.
Every gift that arrives is printed in the black window with its exact
name, even ones you haven't mapped, so you can copy names from there.
Spelling and capital letters of effect names are forgiven a little, but
the bridge window tells you if something doesn't match.

Effects (use these names on the right side):

  heal_full      Full heal
  heal_1         +1 heart
  hurt_2         -2 hearts (never kills)
  hp_1           Down to 1 heart
  magic_full     Refill magic
  magic_drain    Drain magic
  rupees_plus    +50 rupees
  rupees_minus   -50 rupees
  bombs_plus     +10 bombs
  bombs_none     Lose all bombs
  arrows_plus    +10 arrows
  arrows_none    Lose all arrows

  Timed (more gifts add more time):
  invert         Inverted D-pad, 20s
  no_sword       No sword, 20s
  no_item        No Y item, 20s
  swap_by        B and Y swapped, 20s
  one_hit        One-heart mode, 30s
  no_magic       Magic locked, 30s
  ice            Ice physics (slippery floors), 20s
  cucco_storm    Cucco attack, 30s of outdoor time
                 (pauses indoors, picks up again outside)

  App actions:
  sprite_roll    Random Link sprite
  sprite_fav     Random sprite from your starred favourites
  msu_roll       Random MSU-1 music pack from your starred packs
  seed_roll      NEW SEED (needs "Allow seed reroll" ticked)

Other settings in mapping.json:
  "likes": { "every": 300, "effect": "heal_1" }   one effect per 300 likes
  "follow" / "share"     effect for a follow / share (once per viewer
                         per 10 minutes)
  "maxPerGift": 5        cap for gift streaks (20 Roses = 5 heals)


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
- "can't reach TikFinity on port 21213"
    TikFinity isn't open, isn't logged in, or its port isn't 21213.

- The game says "TikFinity, not the bridge: use ws://localhost:21214"
    The game's Bridge address is 21213. Change it to 21214.

- Game status stays "waiting for bridge"
    The black window isn't open, or the address isn't ws://localhost:21214.
    Allow local network access if the browser asked.

- "Node.js is not installed"
    Install it from nodejs.org, then close and reopen the folder.

- A gift shows "not mapped"
    Add its exact name to "gifts" in mapping.json.

- An effect says it "needs a randomizer ROM"
    Ice physics and Cucco attack use features built into randomizer seeds.
    Generate a seed in the game's Randomizer panel.

- Cucco attack seems to do nothing
    It only happens outdoors. Indoors its timer pauses.

Mac / Linux: open a terminal in this folder and run:  node bridge.mjs
