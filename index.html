More Kara settings

Settings, presets and help text (hover a setting) are from Kara's alttpr.gwaa.kiwi. Her generator (alttpr-python, MIT license, built on Aerinon's Door Randomizer and codemann8's Overworld Randomizer) runs here in your browser. The first seed loads Python (about 15 MB, kept for next time). Big shuffles can take a minute or more.

Customizer Off

alttpr.com's Customizer, made here in your browser with the same code: change the item pool, place items, pick starting equipment and more. Just because you can, doesn't mean you should: some settings make games that can't be finished. To keep a setup, use Save in Saved presets: it keeps the bar's settings and these menus.

ROM options
MSU-1 music
MSU Off

MSU-1: choose the pack as one .zip, or all the .pcm files of an ALttP MSU-1 pack at once (on iPhone, tap Select in the file picker, then select them all), or on a computer pick the pack's folder with Folder…. The pack plays instead of the game's music, right away in the game you're playing (an older seed or Resume too) and on every seed after. Every pack you add is kept in ♪ in the header: switch packs mid-game, star favourites (☆) and roll a random one (🎲).

Generate a seed to start

Pick your settings in the Randomizer bar above, choose your own Japanese 1.0 ALttP ROM with Base ROM (once, it's what new seeds are made from), then press Generate & Play. Already have a rolled seed .sfc, or want the plain game? Use Load ROM… at the top instead. The tracker on the right updates automatically as you play.

Everything runs in your browser. Your ROM is never uploaded.
